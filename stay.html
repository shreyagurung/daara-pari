Gairi Ghar

GAIRI GHAR

Built by hand,
one season at a time.

Gairi Ghar began as a dream of building with the earth beneath our feet. Over three years, it slowly took shape using mud, timber and stone, shaped by local hands and traditional building methods that have long belonged to these mountains.

Today it welcomes those looking for a slower rhythm. Wake to birdsong, brew a cup of tea beneath the skylight, wander through the cardamom fields, cook with produce from the garden, and end the day beneath a sky full of stars.
