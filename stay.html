Mul Ghar

Our Family Home

Mul Ghar is the home my family still lives in today. Wooden floors, old photographs, cups of tea that somehow refill themselves, and the smell of lunch drifting in from the kitchen.

Staying here means becoming part of everyday village life for a little while. Sharing home-cooked meals, conversations over tea, and slow evenings spent together after the day's work is done.
