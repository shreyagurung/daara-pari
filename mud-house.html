The Mud House

Gairi Ghar

When we started building Gairi Ghar, we didn't really know what it would become.
No plans. No designs. No blueprints.
Just the idea that we'd build with whatever we had around us. And that it would be a home.

Nothing was really planned. It was more of a go-with-the-flow build with two dajus from the village, figuring things out as we went.

Slowly.

One day at a time.

Every space in Gairi Ghar began with one simple question

What should a
home feel like?

Run your hands over the mud walls. Walk barefoot on the earthen floor. Shower beneath the open sky. Watch the rain. Fall asleep beneath the stars.

Everything in Gairi Ghar is meant to be

touched,
felt &
experienced.

A home made with nature is never really finished.
It needs to be lived in. To be walked on. To be touched. To breathe alongside the people who live in it.

That's why we recommend staying a little longer.

Long enough to settle into the rhythm of this space.

We've built you a home

All you need to do is pack a bag and come.
